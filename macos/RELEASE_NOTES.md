**Cyberpad for Mac (macOS 13 or later, Apple silicon and Intel).** Turn an old iPad or tablet into a macro pad for your Mac.

## Installing

1. Download **Cyberpad-…dmg** below and open it.
2. Drag **Cyberpad** to **Applications**.
3. The first time, open Cyberpad with **right-click › Open**. If you only get "Cancel", go to
   **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to Cyberpad.
   This is needed because the app is not yet notarised by Apple (no Developer ID).
4. Cyberpad now lives as an icon in the menu bar. Click it for the address, the QR code and the pairing code, and open
   that address in Safari on your tablet.
5. For media and key buttons, macOS asks for **Accessibility**: allow Cyberpad in
   System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility.

The tablet and the Mac must be on the same Wi-Fi network.

## What it does

Everything in the README: buttons for media, sound, apps, links (also in a chosen browser), keys, text, macros and
Shortcuts; widgets (calendar, weather, world clock, timers, now playing, system, photos, terminal); Home Assistant,
MQTT, webhooks and plugins; meeting button; profiles and context pages; themes; the layout editor; English, Dutch and
Spanish.

Your data lives in `~/Library/Application Support/Cyberpad` and stays when you delete the app.
